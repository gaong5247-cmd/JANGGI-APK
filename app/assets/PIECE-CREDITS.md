# Janggi Lab offline piece art – attribution

This application includes 14 wooden-style Chinese-character Janggi piece graphics
(7 blue/Cho + 7 red/Han) derived from Kadagaden's `janggi_wooden/` SVG artwork.

- **Artist and copyright:** Kadagaden (https://github.com/Kadagaden/chess-pieces)
- **Artwork:** https://github.com/Kadagaden/chess-pieces/tree/master/janggi_wooden
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- **Transformations:** Resampled into 220 px transparent PNGs with librsvg.
  Files were renamed to `K A B N R C P` (blue / Cho)
  and `k a b n r c p` (red / Han) for stable engine piece mapping.
- **No endorsement** by the artist is implied.

The assets depict realistic wooden Janggi tokens with printed Hanja, not
photographs of physical game pieces. Windows users may supply their own
photographs as 14 transparent PNG files through the Piece Photos Folder
selector. Rights to those third-party photographs remain with their owners.

CC BY 4.0 terms apply independently to the included piece images.
